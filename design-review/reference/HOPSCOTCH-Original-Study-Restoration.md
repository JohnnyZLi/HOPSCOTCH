histories do not replace current source models. The production implementation
retains current event IDs, timestamps, byte builders and scenario selections.

- `motion.js` restores TCP physical identity and semantic leading fields,
  separate endpoint-owned retained records, and the scene-before-transport flow.
- `review.css` restores field and supporting-surface hierarchy. Explicit
  two-column TCP sequence grouping is a protocol presentation policy; actual
  font/width measurements determine whether that readable group fits.
- `lifecycle-data.ts` supplies current `projectJourneyPacketVisual` and
  `projectJourneyPhysicalState` results for packet events. `source-adapters.js`
  binds the actual selected inner-layer fields and envelope state. Static
  historical fixture contexts without those projections remain explicit semantic
  views and do not fabricate wire values.
- Journey keeps one inner carrier while separate Ethernet/FCS pieces use the
  existing geometry-derived attachment planner. The FCS view denotes the wire
  boundary and supplies no invented checksum bytes.
- Packet material equations, the measured skinny scanner and contact/pacing
  kernels remain shared. Only workspace wallpaper exposure is quieter; the home
  wallpaper remains the accepted entry environment.

carrier continuity through decapsulation/TTL/re-encapsulation, seated outer
geometry, endpoint transfer ownership, heading/field layout, and custom menus.
